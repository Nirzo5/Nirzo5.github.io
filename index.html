Case studies

01 · Matching & retrieval ~50% inference cost reduction

A tiered candidate–job matching funnel

Challenge

Decide which candidates deserve an expensive LLM analysis — cheaply, without losing good matches.

Approach

Built a 1,504-pair benchmark from production logs; compared embeddings and cross-encoders with leave-one-job-out CV.

Outcome

Embeddings → lighter LLM → deep analysis on demand; ruled out filters with no signal before they shipped.

02 · Extraction & NLP 0.97 / 0.87 macro F1, English / Hebrew

Bilingual requirement extraction without neural NER

Challenge

Turn free-text job requirements into six typed fields, in English and Hebrew, on every keystroke.

Approach

Dictionary-first design with LLM fallback; Hebrew prefix handling without stemming; a 116k-form taxonomy derived from ESCO.

Outcome

High precision on real ads, a 0.3 ms dictionary path, and 4 API calls across a full typing session.

03 · Product analytics 96 per-role client reports

Measuring what drives client engagement

Challenge

Measure whether in-product activity translates into client engagement, across a real customer funnel.

Approach

Mixpanel + Postgres attribution with causal windows, a negative control, and a 2-D sensitivity grid.

Outcome

Pinpointed which roles and recruiters drive conversion, and turned the analysis into recurring BI feeds and a client-facing report.

04 · Evaluation science 70 → 39 “partial match” overweighted ~2×

Auditing our own LLM grader

Challenge

Find where and why the requirement grader disagrees with ground truth.

Approach

Directional confusion analysis on 10k+ pairs, an LLM-as-judge error taxonomy, OLS + RidgeCV weight recovery.

Outcome

Exposed a generosity bias behind 73% agreement and separated data problems from prompt problems.

05 · Representation design 0.02 real recall behind an F1 of 0.89

Catching a misleading metric before it shipped

Challenge

Test whether embedding similarity can replace the LLM's per-requirement judgment.

Approach

Two-arm ablation, quantile-matched thresholds and macro-F1, read per class.

Outcome

Showed the headline F1 was a base-rate artifact — saving the team from building on it.

06 · Systems engineering 5.5 s earlier first result (−35%)

Streaming structured extraction

Challenge

Show users the first result before the model finishes writing the document.

Approach

A hand-written incremental JSON parser with per-element Pydantic validation.

Outcome

35% lower perceived latency without changing the model, with every extraction auditable.
